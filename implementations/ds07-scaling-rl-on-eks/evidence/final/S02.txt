$ python rank.py   # rank by ceiling only where the 2-standard-error intervals do not overlap

center (lr 3e-6)  vs  surrogate (no score centering): not ranked: the intervals overlap

$ python rank.py --self-check   # the rule on three made-up ceilings: 0.70 and 0.69 (overlap), 0.55 (clear)
